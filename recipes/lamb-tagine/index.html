Lamb Tagine

Slow-braised with apricots and warm spice, finished with cinnamon-toasted almonds.

Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 3 lb bone-in lamb stew meat or neck, cut into 1½-inch pieces (1360 g)
  • 2½ tsp kosher salt, divided, plus more to taste (7 g)
  • 1¾ cups lamb or chicken stock (420 ml)
  • 5 oz dried apricots (142 g)
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more as needed (27 g)
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced (500 g)
  • 1 tsp tomato paste (6 g)
  • ½ tsp grated fresh ginger (2 g)
  • 2 small cinnamon sticks, used separately (4 g)
  • Large pinch saffron (0.2 g)
  • ½ tsp ground ginger (1 g)
  • ¾ tsp ground turmeric (2 g)
  • ¾ tsp ground black pepper (2 g)
  • ¼ tsp ground cinnamon (0.6 g)
  • Pinch freshly grated nutmeg (0.2 g)
  • ⅓ cup chopped fresh cilantro, divided (5 g)
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter (14 g)
  • ½ cup slivered almonds (54 g)
  • 2 scallions, finely chopped (30 g)
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley (8 g)
  • Fresh lemon juice, to taste
  1. Toss the lamb with 2 tsp (5.6 g) of the salt in a large bowl. Hold at room temperature for an hour, or refrigerate up to a day.
  2. Bring the stock to a boil in a small pot, pull it off the heat, drop in the apricots, and leave them to soften for at least 15 minutes.
  3. Heat the oven to 325°F (165°C). Warm the oil over medium heat in a tagine, Dutch oven, or heavy pot with a tight lid. Brown the lamb in batches with space around each piece, roughly 10 minutes per batch, moving finished pieces to a plate.
  4. Pour off the rendered fat, leaving a thin film in the pot. Add the onions and ¼ tsp (0.7 g) salt and cook until soft, about 8 minutes. Stir in the tomato paste, fresh ginger, one cinnamon stick, saffron, ground ginger, turmeric, pepper, ground cinnamon, and nutmeg, and cook until aromatic, about 2 minutes. Return the lamb with its juices, then add the apricots, stock, and half the cilantro. Seal with foil and the lid (foil is unnecessary in a tagine) and cook in the oven 2½ to 3 hours, turning the meat now and then, until tender. Correct the seasoning.
  5. While it cooks, melt the butter with the second cinnamon stick in a small skillet over medium heat. Add the almonds and ¼ tsp (0.7 g) salt and toast to golden brown, 5 to 7 minutes. Remove the cinnamon stick.
  6. Transfer the lamb and its juices to a platter. Scatter over the almonds along with the skillet butter, then the scallions, parsley, and remaining cilantro. Finish with lemon juice. Serve with flatbread or couscous.
